# One in a Computer

A reverse-trivia revision game for **IB Computer Science** (syllabus for first assessment 2027).

Every prompt has many correct answers. Obvious answers barely sink. Valid answers that few people think of send your virus deeper into the computer, from the open internet down to the CPU's registers.

**Play:** https://far1donet.github.io/absolution/

## Choosing what to revise

Pick any mix of syllabus topics (A1.1 to B4.1) and switch between **SL** and **HL**. HL-only topics and subtopics are labelled. Each dive draws 7 prompts from your selection.
"Today's dive" gives everyone who picks the same topics and level the same prompts; practice dives are random.

## Adding prompts

All prompts live in `prompts.js`, grouped by syllabus topic. Each one lists its accepted answers from most to least common, plus one rare "One in a Computer" gem answer.
